Six yards of living heritage

Surat has long been the beating heart of India’s fabric revolution, where master weavers, dyers and embroiderers have perfected their crafts for generations. Charchita was founded to preserve the sacred art of Indian weaving while evolving its appeal for the modern lifestyle.

From Banarasi Kadwa and Kanjivaram Korvai to Gujarati Patola and Bandhani, every drape carries authentic motifs and centuries-old methods, paired with fresh palettes and lighter fabrics.
